Changeover Standards Check

Before you leave, walk the property as if you were the guest arriving today. Slow down, look from different angles, open what a guest will open and ask yourself: would I be delighted to arrive to this?

First impressions & presentation

Pause. Inspect. Correct anything that needs attention.

Would the property feel fresh, welcoming and ready if you arrived as a paying guest?

Is everything presented neatly, with no unpleasant odours, visible dirt or anything out of place?

Bedrooms & linen

Pause. Inspect. Correct anything that needs attention.

Are the beds crisp, evenly made and inviting, with the correct linen?

Have you closely inspected all bedding for hairs, stains, marks or damage?

Are bedside surfaces, headboards and floor edges free of dust, hairs and debris?

Have you checked beneath and around the beds for anything the last guests left behind?

Bathrooms & showers

Pause. Inspect. Correct anything that needs attention.

Are shower screens truly clear and sparkling, including when viewed from an angle?

Are grout, silicone, shower corners and drains free from mould, limescale and trapped hairs?

Are toilets, basins, taps, mirrors and chrome spotless, including less visible edges and bases?

Are towels and bath mats clean, hair-free and presented to a consistent standard?

Kitchen & appliances

Pause. Inspect. Correct anything that needs attention.

Have you opened the fridge and checked its shelves, drawers, seals and handles for food residue and odours?

Have you looked inside the oven and checked for grease, burnt food and leftover debris?

Have you inspected pans, crockery, glasses, cutlery and utensils for grease or food left by previous guests?

Are cupboard fronts, handles, worktops, splashbacks, sink and appliance surfaces free of grease, smears and crumbs?

Living areas & finishing touches

Pause. Inspect. Correct anything that needs attention.

Are sofas, cushions, dining furniture and decorative items clean, tidy and correctly arranged?

Have you checked floors, corners and under accessible furniture for hairs, dust and debris?

Are doors, handles, switches, remotes, windows and other frequently noticed surfaces clean and presentable?

Final guest-eye inspection

Pause. Inspect. Correct anything that needs attention.

Have you walked through every room after removing your supplies and looked at it from a guest’s point of view?

Have you reported any damage, staining, missing items or maintenance concerns rather than leaving them unmentioned?

If a guest arrived in five minutes, would you confidently put your name to the condition of this property?

Issues to report

Anything damaged, missing, stained, requiring maintenance, or unable to be brought up to standard?
